# Separate competitive completion from tournament closure

Docket separates completion of the competition from closure of tournament operations so public results can be finalized without prematurely ending late Judge feedback, administrative work, or evidence-bound correction workflows.

## Consequences

- Final Results Publication transitions the tournament to Competitive Completion but never closes it automatically.
- Only the Tournament Owner may perform Tournament Closure, preserving the existing single-owner governance boundary.
- Closure becomes eligible only after Final Results Publication, the locked Feedback Deadline, and required operational work.
- The hard feedback window ends exactly seven days after the current published end of the final scheduled competitive round, cannot be extended or shortened, and need not end with every Judge submitting feedback.
- A Closure Readiness Review has non-waivable blockers for unpublished Final Results, an open round, an unexpired Feedback Deadline, and unresolved or unpublished changes that could affect public results.
- Outcome-irrelevant missing panel Ballots, missing or unpublished feedback, unacknowledged notices, and incomplete optional exports or notes are warnings the Owner must complete or acknowledge with a reason.
- Closure ends routine operation without deleting public pages, records, versions, attribution, or audit history.
- Public pages, permission-scoped historical views, audit records, and exports remain readable after closure.
- Ordinary registration, staffing, configuration, schedule, pairing, round, Ballot, feedback, standings, advancement, award, and result mutations are rejected.
- Explicit audited Post-Closure Exceptions remain available for authorized corrections and republication, Ownership Recovery, and legally required privacy actions under their existing permissions; official-tournament Feedback cannot reopen after its deadline.
- An authorized correction creates a scoped Post-Closure Correction Case while the tournament remains Closed and all unrelated command guards stay active.
- Public projections retain the last published version until a corrected version completes its normal approval and republication workflow.
- Resolving, rejecting, or withdrawing the case closes only the case and requires no second Tournament Closure action.
