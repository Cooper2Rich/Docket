# Require Two-Person Legal Hold Control

Docket treats a Legal Hold as a separate platform legal and privacy operation rather than an extension of generic administration or tournament authority. Because a hold suspends promised deletion of sensitive data, no single administrator may create, expand, extend, or release one alone.

## Consequences

- Legal and Privacy Operations is a platform permission separate from generic Platform Administrator status.
- Tournament and Accommodation Operations actors have no Legal Hold authority or access to hold details.
- One authorized administrator prepares a proposal and a second distinct authorized administrator approves it before activation.
- Every proposal identifies the legal basis, external case reference, exact record scope, start time, and a review date no more than 90 days away.
- Scope expansion, extension, and release use the same distinct-preparer-and-approver control.
- Review creates an attributed version and cannot silently broaden scope.
- An overdue hold remains active to prevent accidental deletion while Docket escalates it continuously until review.
- Release immediately resumes suspended deletion, including deletion already past due, without tournament approval.
- Hold details are restricted to Legal and Privacy Operations; an affected School sees only whether deletion is legally paused or has resumed.
- Public and tournament views expose neither the hold nor its status.
- Legal Hold actions do not reopen a Closed tournament or grant unrelated operational authority.
