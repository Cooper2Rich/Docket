# Use one school manager with composable membership roles

Each School will have exactly one School Manager while other School Membership roles may be held by multiple Coaches and combined on one membership. This provides one accountable membership authority without forcing roster, entry, and Judge work onto one person.

## Consequences

- Every School has exactly one School Manager and cannot have zero or multiple simultaneous School Managers.
- One Coach may hold School Manager, Roster Manager, Entry Manager, and Judge Manager at the same time.
- Roster Manager, Entry Manager, and Judge Manager are non-exclusive and may be held by multiple Coaches.
- Only the School Manager may invite or revoke School Memberships and assign non-Manager School roles.
- No Coach may grant or delegate the School Manager role except through the controlled transfer or recovery process.
- Revocation prevents future actions without erasing historical attribution.
- A voluntary School Manager Transfer selects an existing School Member, requires recipient acceptance, replaces the Manager atomically, and creates a permanent audit record.
- Loss of access uses normal account recovery before School Manager Recovery is considered.
- School Manager Recovery requires a request from an existing School Member, institutional evidence, notification to the current Manager, approval by two distinct Platform Administrators, recipient acceptance, and a permanent audit record.
- While recovery is pending, Platform Administrators may perform specific attributed Support Actions but cannot create a temporary or second School Manager.
