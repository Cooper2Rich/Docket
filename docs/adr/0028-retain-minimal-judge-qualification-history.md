# Retain Minimal Judge Qualification History

Docket separates raw assessment evidence from the minimal qualification history needed to explain platform qualification and tournament assignment. This prevents immutable attempt history from becoming indefinite storage of answers, detailed scores, integrity evidence, and judging-philosophy text.

## Consequences

- A current Qualification Summary remains while the Event Qualification is valid.
- Full-assessment answers, detailed scores, Critical Competency detail, provider payload, and Assessment Integrity Review evidence are deleted two years after attempt completion unless held.
- An expired or superseded Event Qualification leaves a minimal Qualification History Summary for seven years from expiration or supersession.
- The minimal history contains Judge, event, assessment version, completion date, resulting tier, validity, source, and aggregate critical-item pass or fail, but no answers, question-level outcomes, detailed score, profile text, provider payload, or integrity evidence.
- The current Judging Profile remains until the Judge replaces or deletes it.
- A superseded profile is deleted after two years unless held.
- An exact Published Judging Profile version actually disclosed for a tournament remains restricted Competitive Evidence for seven years after that tournament closes.
- Quick Assessment detail retains its earlier full-completion or missed-follow-up deletion trigger; minimal assignment provenance follows the tournament's seven-year evidence period.
- Every attempt and profile version is immutable until its scheduled deletion.
- Retention never makes assessment data public or grants unrelated Schools, Judges, or tournament personnel access.
- Deletion preserves only minimal historical assignment provenance and never rewrites the assignment itself.
