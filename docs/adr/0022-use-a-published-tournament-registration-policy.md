# Use a published Tournament Registration Policy

Docket governs ordinary registration timing through a versioned Tournament Registration Policy published before registration opens. This gives Schools predictable deadlines while preserving a narrow Director exception for legitimate late registration work and never preventing a School from withdrawing an Entry that cannot compete.

## Consequences

- The Policy contains the registration opening, Entry submission deadline, School edit deadline, admission-decision deadline, and waitlist-closing deadline in a named tournament timezone.
- Its Admission Policy publishes event capacity, optional per-School caps, and objective named priority groups before registration opens.
- Within a priority group, complete submission time orders Entries and a retained reproducible seed resolves exact ties.
- Docket validates a coherent timeline, stores unambiguous instants internally, and preserves every published version.
- Before registration opens, a Director may replace and republish the Policy.
- After opening, a Director may revise only by extending a deadline or relaxing a requirement; shortening, tightening, or a timezone correction that creates an earlier instant is prohibited.
- Each post-opening revision requires an impact preview, preserved prior version, public change notice, and notifications to affected School Members.
- After a deadline but before first-round Pairing Publication, only a Director may approve a one-Entry Late Registration Exception with a reason and impact preview.
- A Late Registration Exception grants only the declared command and cannot alter other Schools' deadlines or bypass data, eligibility, capacity, or validation rules.
- No Late Registration Exception is available after first-round Pairing Publication.
- School Entry Managers may withdraw at any time; a withdrawal after the School edit deadline is a Late Withdrawal with provenance, impact preview, notices, and downstream processing rather than a blocked command.
- Late Withdrawal does not itself create a penalty or disqualification.
- First-round Pairing Publication ends every Registration Policy Revision.
- Docket calculates immutable Admission Recommendations; Registration-authorized staff or a Director confirms only the exact calculated transitions.
- Manual waitlist reordering, lower-ranked selection, seed alteration, and unpublished priority treatment are prohibited.
- Withdrawals and valid capacity increases recalculate the highest-ranked eligible promotion before waitlist closing.
- At waitlist closing, remaining Waitlisted Entries receive a confirmable standardized capacity rejection without exposing private priority evidence, timestamps, seeds, or positions publicly.
