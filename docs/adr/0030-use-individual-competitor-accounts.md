# Use individual Competitor Accounts

In Docket's full production capacity, each Competitor is an individual authenticated Docket Account rather than a School-owned person record. This gives the student a stable identity across tournaments and enables direct participant access, while School representation remains a separate tournament and roster relationship whose authorization rules must be designed explicitly.

Only a School Roster Manager may initiate a Competitor School Invitation, and only the identified Competitor may accept it; email-domain matching and Competitor-initiated affiliation are prohibited. The invitation is bound to the exact verified Google email, delivered by email and the private Account inbox, expires after seven days, and cannot be retargeted. One Competitor Account has exactly one current School Affiliation. Accepting a destination School's invitation replaces the current affiliation without creating a new Account.

The Competitor's profile settings, personal tournament history, and their own authorized Feedback follow the Account across School transfer. Historical Represented-School Tournament Records retain the School represented at the time and include operated Entries and rosters, Pairings, School-directed notices, authorized Ballots and Feedback, released points, results, and corrections. Current Coaching Staff and the School Manager for that represented School retain its still-available records, and the Competitor cannot revoke that School authority; governed privacy and safety restrictions still apply. The destination School receives only information needed for affiliation and records from Entries representing it. Opponent-private information, Judge assessment data, safety evidence, staff investigations, former-School private notes, attestations, accommodations, communications, and unrelated internal records never transfer.

Transfer is immediate for future activity, but an existing Entry continues to represent its recorded School through that tournament and prevents the Competitor from representing both Schools there. Docket notifies the Competitor and authorized Managers of both Schools.

Corrections to a historical Entry notify the Competitor and current Coaching Staff and School Manager of the School represented by that Entry; the destination School is notified only if one of its represented Entries changes. Neither School nor Competitor may erase retention-protected represented-School evidence early. The represented School's Manager holds School Data Export Permission by default and may grant or revoke it for selected current Coaching Staff.

Represented-School export uses a native, permission-aware Docket checklist rather than a packet request. Its fixed School record categories and tournament, season, event, Competitor, record-type, date-range, and format filters cannot expose arbitrary fields or private evidence. CSV is structured and PDF human-readable. One file at or below two gigabytes downloads directly; multiple or oversized output uses ZIP. A single PDF appends its signed integrity page, while CSV is accompanied by a signed PDF manifest inside ZIP. A Docket-signed manifest records the selection, formats, file checksums, counts, and any ZIP sequence; verification keys remain available indefinitely. Exact already-drafted-and-downloaded status includes source versions, filenames follow the fixed convention, CSV schemas are versioned, and formula-triggering user content is neutralized. Fresh Google reauthentication is required for generation and every new or resumed download without ending the ordinary Docket session; failure denies only that attempt. One active job runs per School and later jobs queue without a daily completion cap. Five user-cancelled or non-system failed jobs in sixty minutes create a one-hour generation-only throttle, while system failures do not count and completed downloads remain available. Cancellation preserves payload-free history and warns that downloaded copies cannot be recalled. School Data Export Permission alone does not expose the Manager's all-School Export History.

School Data Export Permission is indivisible and non-redelegable. A grant requires fresh Manager reauthentication, consequences review, attribution, recipient notice, and recipient acceptance under current Google authentication within seven days. It ends on revocation or loss of Coaching Staff role or School Membership. Accepted grants survive Manager succession under a persistent seven-day review. Revocation cancels the actor's pending jobs and live or shared downloads without recalling external copies. A permissioned Coach sees only their own two-year activity; the Manager sees all School history. The creator or Manager may share a generated export only with another current permission holder. Private presets disable without transfer on role loss, while Manager-published configuration-only presets survive transitions until retired. Generated files are immutable and source corrections mark them outdated and notify still-authorized downloaders during the delivery-record window. Stable Docket IDs, minimized personal identifiers, ISO timestamps, tournament-timezone context, visible no-tracking attribution, searchable structured PDFs, and verification without record upload apply throughout.

An unreviewed inherited grant suspends after seven days until a freshly reauthenticated successor decision; declined or expired offers close and must be reissued. Coaching Staff may submit an expiring explanation request without receiving authority. Shares end at the earliest of export expiry, explicit revocation, or recipient permission loss, while the Manager retains remaining-window access to completed School output after creator role loss. Manager-published preset edits create attributed versions retained for two years. Generation pins one source-version snapshot, historical rows preserve the Entry ID and represented School, and the first release excludes contact-information exports. A former lawful recipient may validate the file signature with public keys, but current School authority is required for live outdated status.

School Entry Managers remain responsible for preparing, submitting, updating, and withdrawing Entries. A Competitor confirms identity, represented School, and event before first submission and may report an error, but cannot independently submit, withdraw, accept, or reject an Entry. Account suspension blocks the Competitor's access without withdrawing an Entry or deciding a competitive outcome. For a minor Competitor, an authorized School actor must record a minimized one-school-year authorization attesting that the required permission and consent exist; Google authentication is not consent.

Record-only Competitors may be uploaded solely as non-production test fixtures while the software is being exercised. A test fixture does not establish a production identity, authentication, School authority, or migration shortcut and cannot appear in a production tournament.

This supersedes ADR 0002 and ADR 0003. Existing decisions about preserving historical Entry School attribution and preventing cross-School private-data disclosure remain goals, but their School-owned-record mechanism is no longer the production model.
