# Separate Judge competency from judging profile

The Full Judge Qualification Assessment will combine a scored Judge Competency Section with an unscored Judging Profile. It is required for debate unless an authorized Quick Judge Assessment supplies tournament-day eligibility, and optional but recommended for speech. Competency determines the supported Judge Qualification Tier. The profile describes how a Judge evaluates arguments and may improve assignment fit, but legitimate judging preferences do not reduce competency scores or tiers.

## Consequences

- Debate Judges complete the full assessment or an authorized Quick Judge Assessment before assignment. Speech Judges may be assigned without an assessment, though completion is recommended.
- When tournament staffing requires moving a Judge into an unqualified debate event, a Director or Judge-and-room-authorized staff member may authorize a Quick Judge Assessment whose Temporary Debate Qualification lasts only for the tournament days.
- After the tournament, Docket notifies the Judge when the full assessment is due to avoid another quick assessment and when detailed quick data will be deleted; minimal assignment-audit metadata remains.
- With no future debate tournament, the Quick Assessment Follow-up Deadline is 30 days after the prior tournament with reminders on days 14 and 27. With a future debate tournament, it becomes two days before that tournament, even when later than 30 days.
- Missing the deadline deletes the prior detailed quick data and requires a new Quick Judge Assessment for the next debate tournament; Temporary Debate Qualifications never transfer between tournaments.
- The Full Judge Qualification Assessment captures new responses and a new Judging Profile without importing or merging Quick Assessment content. Completing it makes the full result authoritative and immediately deletes the detailed quick score, responses, and profile while retaining minimal evidence for assignments that previously relied on the temporary qualification.
- Each full result creates an Event Qualification for one debate or speech event. A new debate event requires the full or quick assessment; speech assessment remains optional.
- A current Event Qualification is reusable across tournaments using compatible Ruleset versions.
- An Event Qualification remains current for one competitive season unless a material event-Ruleset or assessment change supersedes it earlier.
- Editorial changes do not trigger reassessment, and expired or superseded qualifications remain immutable evidence for historical assignments.
- A tournament may require a separate Tournament Judge Orientation for its overrides and local procedures; orientation completion affects tournament eligibility but not the Judge's tier.
- The Judge Competency Section tests applicable rules, ethics, and argument evaluation.
- Docket automatically assigns tiers from versioned, published Tier Scoring Bands, and every required Critical Competency Item must be correct before any passing tier is issued.
- Tier Scoring Bands, Critical Competency Items, and tier meanings are platform-controlled and calibrated through pilot testing; tournaments cannot customize them.
- Tournaments may set Round Tier Requirements and may issue explicit Tournament Tier Grants, but cannot alter a Judge's assessment score or Event Qualification tier.
- A Judge permanently improves an Event Qualification tier only by testing into a higher scoring band. A Tournament Tier Grant creates a separate Effective Tournament Tier for the current tournament and expires without changing the external score or tier.
- A Director or Judge-and-room-authorized Tabulation Staff member may issue any higher Tournament Tier Grant at their discretion despite a nonpassing assessment or failed Critical Competency Item; this explicitly supersedes the proposed local qualification floor while leaving the platform result unchanged.
- Assessment results, scores, Critical Competency Item outcomes, and tiers cannot be appealed or corrected by staff. A different outcome may come only from a new assessment attempt under the retake or test-out rules.
- The Judging Profile records accepted differences in judging philosophy and interaction with arguments.
- Competency and profile data are stored and evaluated separately.
- Judge-Use scheduling uses tier as the qualification signal and may use profile data only as a secondary fit signal.
- Docket does not infer competency from prior competitors' win rates, judge popularity, or expressed legitimate argument preferences.
- Judges may view all of their own assessment data, excluding protected platform answer keys, unpublished question banks, and another Judge's data.
- Directors and Judge-and-room-authorized Tabulation Staff may view operational qualification status, tiers, validity, grant history, and the Published Judging Profile, but not raw scores or detailed responses.
- Active School Members for the assigned Entries may view only the assigned Judge's Published Judging Profile after Pairing Publication. Other Judges, unrelated Schools, and Public Viewers receive no access.
- Detailed assessment evidence is available to authorized Platform Administrators only within an attributed and audited Assessment Integrity Review. It may investigate provider delivery, identity, security, or administration but cannot alter the result.
- A failed full assessment requires event-specific remediation and a 24-hour wait before a randomized equivalent retake, with no more than two retakes in a rolling 30-day period.
- A currently qualified Judge may make one Tier Test-Out Attempt every 30 days. Only a higher valid result upgrades the tier; a lower or failed result leaves the current Event Qualification unchanged.
- Every attempt remains immutable while retained, and Docket uses the highest current valid result. Retention and deletion follow ADR 0028.
- Docket provides no assessment accessibility accommodations. Every Judge receives the same timing, delivery configuration, content blueprint, critical-item requirements, scoring bands, and tier standards for a given event-specific assessment version.
- Equivalent randomized forms remain permitted for retakes and test security when they apply the same blueprint and standards.
- Questions, score calculation, ranking, tier thresholds, calibration, detailed compatibility rules, and integrity-review authority are deferred to the separate linked assessment product rather than decided in the current Docket backend milestone.
