# Separate competitive results from ballot feedback

Docket will lock a Ballot's Competitive Result at submission while allowing the Judge to add or revise Ballot Feedback afterward without staff reopening. This lets tabulation advance immediately without forcing Judges to choose between timely decisions and useful written feedback.

## Consequences

- Competitive Result and Ballot Feedback have separate state and revision histories.
- Ballot Submission validates and locks the Competitive Result even when Ballot Feedback is incomplete or absent.
- An identical authenticated Ballot Submission retry returns the original receipt and creates no duplicate Ballot or result version.
- A post-lock submission with different competitive data is rejected as a Conflicting Ballot Attempt, retained in restricted audit history, and reported to the Judge and authorized Ballot-and-correction actors.
- Pairing, standings, and advancement may consume the Competitive Result immediately after submission.
- Changing or disputing a submitted Competitive Result requires an authorized, reasoned, audited Ballot Reopening; a conflicting submission never silently overwrites or supersedes it.
- Feedback additions and revisions do not require Ballot Reopening, but retain version history and actor attribution.
- The hard Feedback Deadline is exactly seven days after the current published end of the final scheduled competitive round and cannot be extended or shortened by any actor or configuration.
- At the Feedback Deadline, every official-tournament Feedback version becomes immutable; no actor or Post-Closure Exception may reopen it.
- An authorized pre-end Schedule revision recalculates the deadline from the revised final scheduled competitive round's published end; this is an anchor change, not deadline discretion.
- Expiration of the Feedback Deadline permits Tournament Closure without requiring every Judge to submit or publish feedback.
- A Feedback Draft is visible only to its Judge until explicit Feedback Publication.
- Feedback Publication makes one version visible to the adjudicated Competitors, every current Coaching Staff member for each Competitor's School, and that School's sole School Manager; Ballot Feedback is never public in the first slice.
- Later edits create a private draft while recipients continue seeing the last Published Feedback.
- An unpublished draft remains private and is flagged incomplete at the Feedback Deadline rather than being published automatically.
- Safety personnel may hide or restrict harmful Published Feedback after finality without editing or reopening it, while preserving the exact version as restricted evidence.
- Recipient authorization may be corrected after finality without changing Feedback content; Docket preserves delivery history and privately notifies affected people.
- A corrected released point updates the Competitor and their authorized School recipients together, preserves prior restricted values, and becomes the only value consumed by standings.
- Feedback and released-point access belongs to active Coaching Staff Memberships for the Competitor's School and the sole School Manager; removing that authority ends Docket access immediately.
- Rubric-version notices and owner acknowledgment are informational and never block Practice Role Lock, round start, or session start.
- Starting a round creates an immutable Round Rubric Pin. Every Ballot and decision for that round remains tied to the exact version even if a defect is discovered before submission.
- Emergency Rubric Publication may use a fifteen-minute qualified-publisher request window only to correct future unstarted rounds; it cannot migrate or rewrite a started round.
- The first authorized Ready-or-Scheduled-to-Started transition in a scheduled event-round cohort creates one Round Rubric Pin for every pairing and room in that cohort; scheduled time and Ballot opening do not create it.
- Missing rubric acknowledgment or failed emergency notice delivery is nonblocking. Docket records a warning, retries and escalates delivery, and prominently displays the governing version at round entry.
- A Rubric-Blocked Decision pauses Judge submission and routes the round to an evidence-backed Director Administrative Ruling without changing the pin or fabricating a Judge Ballot.
- That ruling normally requires verification by a distinct Ballot-correction-authorized Tabulation Staff member; a warned, reasoned, evidence-reviewed Director-only exception is allowed when no second actor exists.
- Its restricted evidence package contains the pinned version, affected fields, Judge report, validation failure, impossibility explanation, governing Ruleset and Pairing, proposed outcome, and downstream preview, excluding unrelated private evidence.
- The Director-only exception requires a recorded availability check showing no verifier is reasonably available before the result is operationally required; convenience and ordinary delay are insufficient.
- The Director must publish or escalate the ruling within thirty minutes of the report and before the next affected event round, whichever comes first. Expiry creates no automatic outcome, blocks only dependent standings, Pairings, advancement, and publication, and repeatedly alerts every Director, the Owner, and Ballot-correction-authorized Tabulation Staff until publication or formal assignment.
- Formal assignment requires one Director to accept responsibility in Docket with actor and time; an audited transfer may replace that Director, and assisting Tabulation Staff does not acquire ruling authority.
- Affected Competitors, Coaches, and the assigned Judge see only Administrative Review in Progress while the overdue ruling remains unresolved.
- Only an affected Competitor, their current represented-School Coach or Manager, the assigned Judge, or authorized tournament staff may file a participant correction request, and the window closes sixty minutes after notice or at the next affected event round. Afterward, Ballot-correction-authorized Tabulation Staff may open and prepare an objective correction, but only a Director may approve or reject it.
- Docket may flag a system defect but cannot correct it automatically. A potentially outcome-changing flag blocks only dependent artifacts pending dismissal or case opening; a lower-impact flag warns without blocking.
- A corrected ruling sends audience-filtered private notices to affected Competitors, represented-School Coaching Staff and Managers, the assigned Judge, Directors, and involved Tabulation Staff.
- Failed notice delivery never reverses a correction; Docket retries in-app and email delivery, preserves the inbox notice, and escalates repeated failure.
- The outcome is labeled Administrative Ruling — Rubric Defect, exposes only its general reason and outcome where the round decision ordinarily appears, and creates no Judge lateness, hold, No-Show, reliability, or qualification consequence.
- Unaffected pairings continue under the cohort pin, and a corrected version may govern the whole cohort only while none of its pairings has started.
- An Unacknowledged Rubric Notice clears actively on acknowledgment but remains as minimized restricted operational telemetry for two years after acknowledgment or closure and is visible only to the responsible Director or owner and rules-governance administrators.
- Protected Feedback and point access is revalidated on every request and active session. Role loss closes the view and blocks later exports without claiming to erase an external copy already downloaded.
- Historical School-scoped Feedback follows the School represented by its Entry, while the Competitor retains personal history and a destination School receives only its own represented Entries.
- Restricted exports are visibly attributed without remote tracking, and safety-restricted Feedback is excluded from ordinary Account and School exports.
- The authoring Judge or an affected Competitor may request one safety reconsideration within fourteen days by different Platform Safety personnel; content stays restricted and no Competitive Result may change.
- Reconsideration outcome notices expose only Restored, Remains Restricted, or Restricted with Revised Scope to affected recipients; evidence and reviewer reasoning remain private.
- Represented-School Tournament Records include operated Entries and rosters, Pairings, School notices, authorized Ballots and Feedback, released points, results, and corrections while excluding opponent-private and restricted assessment, safety, and investigation material.
- A Competitor cannot revoke the represented School's historical authority, and ordinary role removal does not turn a legitimately authorized prior export into a security incident.
- Represented-School export is a native Docket checklist workflow, not a packet request. The School Manager holds School Data Export Permission by default and may grant or revoke it for selected current Coaching Staff; general Coaching Staff membership alone grants nothing.
- The permission is indivisible and non-redelegable. A grant requires fresh Manager Google reauthentication, consequences review, attribution, recipient notice, and in-app acceptance under current Google authentication within seven days.
- A grant ends immediately on Manager revocation, Coaching Staff role loss, or School Membership loss. Manager succession preserves accepted grants under a persistent seven-day successor review.
- An unreviewed inherited grant suspends at the nonextendable seven-day deadline and can resume only through the current successor Manager's freshly reauthenticated confirmation. Suspension survives further Manager transitions and immediately notifies the affected Coach and Manager; failed email is retried three times over 24 hours, retained in-app, and escalated to the Manager without changing suspension. Confirmation reuses the recipient's original acceptance only while their Coaching Staff role and School Membership remain valid, then notifies both parties. Restoration returns access to still-available previously generated exports under current authorization, fresh Google reauthentication, and applicable outdated warnings; expiry remains final. Canceled jobs stay terminal and disabled shares do not revive, so the Coach must start new jobs and the creator or Manager must issue any new share after rechecking recipients.
- Coaching Staff may submit a seven-day explanatory permission request, but it grants no authority and can only lead to the ordinary Manager workflow. Request content and decisions are restricted to the requester, current Manager, and governed privacy or audit actors. Role or Membership loss closes it as ineligible without transfer; the former requester retains only their own explanation, final status, and Manager decision reason for the same two-year history period. A denial requires a requester-visible reason; the Manager decides and explains any materially-different-request bypass. Three or more bypasses in 90 days warn the Manager and create governed audit review of request metadata and explanations without automatically blocking authority or exposing export contents; broader action requires a separately authorized investigation. Governed reviewers close the flag as `Expected Use`, `Policy Concern`, or `Investigation Required`, notify the Manager, retain the classification for two years, and notify a requester only if their own permission changes. `Policy Concern` adds a persistent Manager warning and required acknowledgment but no restriction. `Investigation Required` creates a restricted referral requiring approval by a second governed audit or privacy actor before a full investigation opens. Manager succession transfers the original request and deadline and gives the successor access to the School's retained two-year request history. Denial or withdrawal has no separate appeal and blocks same-purpose refiling for thirty days unless circumstances materially change.
- The permission-aware checklist contains only Entries, operated rosters, Pairings, School notices, authorized Ballots and Feedback, released points, results, and Correction Notices, filterable by tournament, season, event, Competitor, record type, date range, and CSV or PDF. Arbitrary fields and private evidence are unavailable.
- CSV is structured and PDF is human-readable while both represent the same authorized facts. One file at or below two gigabytes downloads directly; multiple or oversized output uses ZIP. A single PDF appends its signed integrity page, while CSV is accompanied by a signed PDF manifest in ZIP.
- Historical dashboard search is metadata-only across tournament, season, event, Competitor, record type, and represented School; private Feedback is not globally full-text indexed for School users.
- Restored safety-restricted Feedback returns to ordinary authorized views and future exports without automatically generating or sending a file.
- Neither a School nor Competitor may delete represented-School evidence before retention expires, although correction, restriction, safety review, and legally required privacy actions remain.
- Docket signs a manifest containing the selection, formats, checksums, record counts, and any ZIP-part sequence; non-secret verification keys and rotation history remain indefinitely.
- An authenticated pre-download summary exposes the date range, included and excluded categories, file count, total size, and retention warnings. Prior-download status requires an exact School, selection, filters, format, and source-version match; changed sources mark the earlier output outdated.
- Filenames identify Docket, School, tournament or range, category, and generation date, with Competitor identity only for intentionally single-Competitor output. CSV schemas have stable versioned columns, and formula-triggering user content is neutralized.
- One generation job runs per School and later jobs queue without a daily completion cap. Five user-cancelled or non-system failed jobs in sixty minutes pause new generation for one hour; system failures do not count, and dashboards and completed downloads remain usable.
- Generation and every new or resumed file or ZIP-part download require fresh Google reauthentication without ending the ordinary Docket session or creating a separate password. Reauthentication failure affects only that attempt. Cancellation removes remaining server-side files and preserves payload-free history and source retention; Docket warns that downloaded copies cannot be recalled.
- Revocation cancels the actor's pending generation and live or shared downloads. An export creator or Manager may share a generated file only with another current permission holder, who reauthenticates independently; audit history remains after access ends.
- A share ends on file expiry, creator or Manager revocation, or recipient permission loss. Creator role loss still permits the Manager to download a completed School file during its remaining delivery window.
- Generated files are immutable. A source correction marks them outdated, identifies only changed categories, and notifies only still-authorized actual downloaders during the seven-day delivery-record window without attaching data or regenerating automatically. Each new or resumed outdated download session requires fresh reauthentication and one explicit warning acknowledgment covering its listed parts; School Export History records the acknowledgment without changing the signed artifact.
- A replacement is a separate job created only after copying the prior selection and filters into a new review and receiving explicit confirmation. Canceling it leaves the older outdated export available for its remaining original window, and starting or awaiting a replacement never extends that window. A compromised-key replacement uses the current authorized source snapshot, links to the affected artifact in history, and identifies changed record categories rather than attempting byte-for-byte reproduction.
- Permissioned staff may retain private configuration-only presets while authorized; role loss disables them without transfer. Manager-published School presets survive staff and Manager transitions until a current Manager retires them. A broken schema mapping blocks generation until the private owner or School Manager, respectively, explicitly removes or replaces each affected field and saves a new attributed version; Docket recommendations never apply automatically. The new version records its predecessor, editor, time, removed fields, chosen replacements, rejected suggestions, and resulting schema version without exported data.
- A Manager-published preset revision creates a new attributed current version and retains earlier versions for two years in Manager history.
- Exports use stable Docket IDs, authorized labels, ISO 8601 timestamps and tournament-timezone context while excluding email, Google, authentication, and unrelated contact identifiers absent separate authority. Visible recipient and source attribution contains no tracking, PDFs remain searchable and structured, and signature verification uploads no private record content.
- Generation pins one source-version snapshot, so concurrent corrections mark the completed output outdated rather than mix versions. Each of no more than three consistency retries pins the newest complete snapshot at retry start, and the manifest identifies only the successful snapshot. Authorization and retention are revalidated at every attempt; if required records were deleted, generation fails with category-level categories, approximate counts, and governing retention reasons, never deleted contents, opponent information, private evidence, or stale attempt data. Exhaustion fails and notifies without counting toward abuse throttling. Historical rows retain the Entry ID and School represented at that time, and the first release has no contact-information export category.
- Multipart delivery records each successfully delivered part and marks an interrupted session incomplete. Resuming requests only missing parts and requires fresh Google reauthentication plus another outdated warning when applicable. A checksum failure invalidates only the affected part, prevents the complete-export status, and permits an authenticated redownload during availability. After two failed redownloads of the same part, Docket stops serving it, marks the artifact unavailable, notifies requester and Manager, and opens a **School Export Integrity Review**. A designated-security Platform Administrator independent of the generation actor must classify possible broader compromise within 24 hours; School and tournament staff may submit evidence but cannot decide. Overdue review persistently escalates without automatic restoration or replacement. Staff cannot repair signed files in place: review either confirms artifact invalidation and offers new authorized generation or escalates evidence of wider compromise while preserving history. Invalidation notifies requester, current Manager, and actual downloaders; former downloaders receive only artifact identity, invalid status, and safe next steps. A continuously connected transfer authorized before expiry may finish unless School authority is revoked, which terminates it immediately. Already downloaded parts cannot be recalled, so Docket records them, stops remaining transfer, and warns the Manager that external copies may exist. A disconnect after expiry cannot resume; expiry blocks every new request and never extends server-side storage.
- A former lawful recipient may verify a signature using public keys without regaining School access; live outdated status remains restricted to current School authority. A signature from a later-revoked or compromised key reports cryptographic validity, key status, revocation date, and a review warning without School or export content. A safely retired key remains valid and trusted and reports its retirement date without a compromise warning. Public verification requires no Account, uses privacy-preserving rate limits, and retains no submitted file, manifest, checksum, or identifying export metadata after the check. Docket launches both a public web verifier and downloadable offline utility using one versioned validation specification, identical result behavior, and a shared signed test-vector set that every release must pass. Utility releases and update manifests are signed through Docket's trust chain, and the utility rejects unsigned or incorrectly signed updates. Docket publishes signed, downloadable and versioned verification-key directory snapshots containing active and retired keys, rotation history, revocation status, and effective dates without School or recipient data; offline tools display snapshot publication time and last online revocation check, warn that revocation status may be stale after 24 hours without a successful refresh while still reporting the local result, and do not claim current trust until an online check succeeds. A published trust chain preserves verification across ordinary root rotation and reports the historical root and status without treating rotation as compromise. A separate tightly controlled root key signs the directory. Ordinary root rotation requires two Platform Administrators and publishes the transition. Root compromise uses a predesignated offline recovery quorum with independently controlled credentials, never one administrator, to revoke the root, establish a replacement, publish the transition through independent official Docket channels, and maintain an urgent verification warning until clients trust the new root.
- A multipart export is fully valid only when every listed part passes its checksum and the signed manifest validates the complete ordered set. Missing, duplicated, reordered, or corrupted parts produce `Incomplete` or `Invalid`, never a valid complete result.
- When a signing key is compromised, Docket notifies current Managers and actual downloaders of affected unexpired exports and offers authorized replacement generation without silently changing or re-signing any existing artifact. A former downloader receives only a minimized notice identifying the artifact and signing key, with no current School data or restored access. Failed compromise notice remains in the Docket inbox when available, retries email three times over 24 hours, records delivery status, and leaves the public key directory as the authoritative warning. Compromise replacements receive priority over ordinary queued School exports after the active School job finishes, preserving one active job per School, and their predownload comparison exposes only changed categories and counts outside the newly authorized artifact. When retention prevents an exact count, the comparison labels the category unavailable because of retention and omits the count without estimation.
- A date-range preflight identifies included and excluded retention categories and imminent scheduled deletion without extending retention.
- The current School Manager may view two years of all-School payload-free School Export History; a permissioned Coaching Staff member sees only their own activity. A Manager transition cancels the former Manager's personal pending jobs and downloads while preserving accepted staff grants for the successor's required review.
- A normal Lincoln-Douglas preliminary Competitive Result contains one winning Entry and Ruleset-valid Speaker Points for both Competitors; pairing identities and sides come from the published pairing.
- An Elimination Round Competitive Result contains a winner and no Speaker Points; this applies to individual Ballots, Panel Decisions, Administrative Outcomes, corrections, and disqualifications.
- A multi-Judge panel has an odd size, independent Judge Ballots, and a strict-majority Panel Decision. The result may lock at an Irreversible Majority while remaining Ballots stay due and tracked.
- Ballot Due Time is the scheduled round end plus a tournament-configurable grace period that defaults to 15 minutes.
- Docket reminds an outstanding Judge at scheduled end, marks and notifies the Ballot as Late at its due time, and escalates it to Judge-and-room staff and Directors ten minutes later.
- Escalation places a tournament-local Judge Assignment Hold on new assignments until the missing result is submitted or a Director records clearance; a Director may remove future assignments through normal revision controls.
- A Judge Assignment Hold, late Ballot, or future-assignment removal does not alter assessment data, qualification tier, Judging Profile, or eligibility at another tournament.
- A single-Judge round or panel without an Irreversible Majority blocks result-dependent operations until a result arrives or a Director makes an evidence-backed Administrative Ruling; missing Ballots never create an automatic forfeit or Entry penalty.
- Byes, forfeits, and other Administrative Outcomes use a separate audited staff workflow and are not represented by fabricated Judge Ballots.
- An adjudicated preliminary round retains Judge-submitted Speaker Points by default after a disqualification changes its competitive decision unless the Ruleset explicitly excludes or replaces them.
- An unadjudicated Administrative Outcome has no Judge-submitted Speaker Points. A Ruleset-defined formula may create clearly labeled Administrative Points with full calculation provenance.

The hard seven-day window supersedes the original 72-hour configurable and extension-only models. Tournament Closure cannot shorten an unexpired Feedback window.
