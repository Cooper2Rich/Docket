# Version result corrections and surface downstream conflicts

A Competitive Result correction will create a new version, recompute derived standings and advancement, and preserve the original result. Docket will not silently rewrite later published or completed rounds when a correction changes their inputs; it will surface those differences as Downstream Conflicts for explicit resolution.

## Consequences

- Only a Tournament Director or Tabulation Staff member with the Ballot and correction permission bundle may initiate Ballot Reopening.
- Reopening requires a reason, an impact preview, and explicit confirmation.
- The original Competitive Result remains immutable in history; the accepted Result Correction becomes the current version.
- Derived standings and advancement recompute after correction and preserve their prior revisions for audit.
- Published or completed downstream rounds do not change automatically.
- Each mismatch between corrected inputs and a downstream artifact becomes a Downstream Conflict requiring an explicit, attributed, audited resolution.
- Tabulation Staff may prepare the impact analysis and resolution plan, but only a Tournament Director may approve a Downstream Conflict resolution.
- Unpublished artifacts regenerate from corrected data. Published but unstarted rounds are withdrawn and republished with notices. Started or completed rounds remain as played, with an Administrative Ruling governing effects on future standings and advancement.
- A correction affecting published final results creates a versioned Correction Notice.
- Resolution preserves every original artifact and prior version; it never deletes tournament history.
- The original Judge supplies the Result Correction whenever available.
- If unavailable, authorized staff may enter a Staff Result Correction only with documented Judge confirmation or an Administrative Ruling; the evidence, staff actor, and reason are recorded, and the correction is never attributed to the Judge.
- A Result Correction does not change Ballot Feedback or its publication state.
- Only a Tournament Director may create a Completed Round Decision Correction, and only for a verified input error or disqualification after reviewing evidence, Ruleset authority, and an impact preview.
- The correction creates a new Director-attributed competitive-decision version and recomputes standings and advancement. It does not reopen or alter the Judge's Ballot or Ballot Feedback.
- A completed round's pairing, Entries, sides, Judge, room, schedule block, and Pairing Publication remain immutable.
- Strategic dissatisfaction, preferred advancement, or reconsideration of substantive adjudication cannot justify the correction.
- A Disqualification Ruling uses a Ruleset-supported scope of round-only loss, prospective tournament removal, or full-tournament disqualification.
- Prior decisions remain current unless the Ruleset explicitly requires retroactive forfeits. Each affected completed round then receives its own versioned correction rather than a bulk overwrite.
- Docket previews and records effects on eligibility, standings, advancement, awards, publications, and downstream artifacts before Director confirmation and sends versioned notices afterward.
- Preliminary-round Judge-submitted Speaker Points remain current by default when an adjudicated decision changes for disqualification unless the Ruleset explicitly excludes or replaces them. Elimination Rounds never contain points.
- An unadjudicated forfeit, bye, or other Administrative Outcome contains no Judge-submitted points. Only a Ruleset-defined formula may create Administrative Points, which retain their formula, inputs, version, and non-Judge provenance.
