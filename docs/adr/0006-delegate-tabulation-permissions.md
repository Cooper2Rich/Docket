# Delegate tabulation permissions explicitly

Tabulation Staff will receive explicit, per-tournament operational permission bundles rather than inheriting all Tournament Director authority. This adds authorization detail but preserves least privilege, makes delegations reviewable, and keeps tournament governance distinct from day-to-day tabulation work.

## Consequences

- A Tournament Staff Assignment links one account to one tournament and one or more Tournament Permission Bundles.
- Initial bundles cover registration, pairings, judges and rooms, ballots and corrections, and publication.
- Director appointment, Ruleset Migration, Emergency Amendments, tournament ownership, and tournament closure remain Director-only governance actions.
- Tabulation Staff cannot grant themselves permissions or delegate a bundle they do not control.
- Revocation prevents future operations immediately without erasing historical actor attribution.
