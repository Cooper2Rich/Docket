# Require one owner and allow multiple directors

Every tournament will have exactly one Tournament Owner and may have multiple additional Tournament Directors. This combines a single accountability and succession root with enough Director coverage to operate a tournament reliably.

## Consequences

- The Tournament Owner is always also a Tournament Director, and a tournament cannot have zero or multiple Owners.
- Only the Owner may appoint or remove other Directors, transfer ownership, or close the tournament.
- Final Results Publication by another authorized actor does not close the tournament or broaden the Owner-only closure authority.
- Additional Directors may govern Rulesets, Emergency Amendments, and Tournament Staff Assignments but cannot remove the Owner or transfer ownership.
- Ownership transfer is complete only after the recipient accepts it; the transfer and acceptance remain in the audit record.
- Loss of account access uses normal account recovery before any Ownership Recovery is considered.
- Ownership Recovery requires a request from an existing Director, verified evidence, notification to the current Owner, approval by two distinct Platform Administrators, recipient acceptance, and a permanent audit record.
- During an active tournament, a Platform Administrator may issue an Emergency Authority Grant to an existing Director instead of rushing a permanent transfer; the grant is narrowly scoped, time-limited, and expires automatically.
