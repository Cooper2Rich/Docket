## Delivered slice

- `@docket/tournaments` creates a UUIDv7 Tournament in `draft` with its creating Account as the single active Owner, validates an IANA timezone, persists client-stable command receipts and domain events, and exposes audience-safe authority projections.
- Owners and Directors issue exact-email access offers. Accepted Director assignments carry intrinsic Tournament governance authority; accepted staff assignments carry only explicit operational permission bundles. Owners, Directors, and delegated staff can delegate only within their current authority.
- Migration 000008 adds Tournament, staff-assignment, access-offer, event, and command-receipt storage. Deferred PostgreSQL constraints enforce one matching active Owner at transaction commit, including concurrent low-level writes.
- Fastify exposes Account-scoped creation and offer routes plus Tournament-scoped authority, offer, and permission routes. URL-addressable accessible React screens cover creation, management, denial, empty, busy, error, staff, delegation, inbox, and acceptance states.
- Executable contracts own request and response schemas, lifecycle transitions, authorization rows, stable errors, events, generated client methods, and Release 1 traceability.
