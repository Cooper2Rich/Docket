## Delivered slice

- School membership offers, School affiliation invitations, and Tournament access offers append a restricted `OutboxEnvelope` inside the same module-owned transaction as the source record. Source-owned leased readers publish due envelopes to pg-boss and mark them dispatched only after queue acceptance.
- `@docket/communications` owns idempotent notice intents, delivery attempts, restricted Account inbox items, stable events, retry scheduling, escalation, and Mailpit and SES provider adapters. PostgreSQL row locks and delivery leases serialize duplicate jobs while preserving at-least-once recovery.
- Required email delivery makes at most three attempts: the first transient failure waits one hour, the second waits another 23 hours, and a third or permanent failure escalates. Provider failure never mutates the source access offer.
- Existing Accounts whose verified email matches the recipient receive an inbox item in addition to email. The Account route returns only the authenticated Account's items, exposes a safe routing summary, and routes non-Account notices through context switching before private detail.
- Fastify exposes `ReadAccessInbox`; the generated contract surface owns the schemas, events, authorization rows, errors, and typed client method. The worker process composes source outboxes, pg-boss, communications storage, identity recipient lookup, and the configured Mailpit or SES adapter.

## Delivery semantics

The internal queue, intent creation, attempt records, and inbox writes are idempotent. External email remains an at-least-once effect across a process crash after provider acceptance and before local completion; every provider request carries a stable per-cycle idempotency key for correlation and provider-side suppression where supported. Docket does not claim external exactly-once delivery.
